Short & sweet

MoinPDF is a workspace for editing, checking and converting PDFs and other documents, available in German and English. Paperwork. Sorted.

A little more detailed

MoinPDF brings together versatile tools for everyday work with PDFs and other documents. These include merging, splitting, text recognition, page editing, forms and various conversions. Many tools process files directly in the browser. Built-in documentation explains each tool, its results and its limitations.

More about the use and the individual tools can be found in the Documentation.
